RT2012 regulatory compliance · Pléiades

Thermal sizing and code compliance for a single-family home.

Building envelope and HVAC/DHW sizing for a new 131.20 m² detached house in Pléiades, progressing from a non-compliant baseline to full French RT2012 regulatory compliance.

Context

Verifying energy code compliance at the design stage

France’s RT2012 building energy code enforces three mandatory caps on new builds: bioclimatic heating/cooling/lighting need (Bbio), primary energy consumption (Cep), and maximum indoor summer temperature (Tic)—all governed by insulation levels, thermal bridging, and system efficiencies.

Working from architectural drawings of a 131.20 m² detached house, I modelled the building in Pléiades. An initial baseline run quantified fabric heat losses and pinpointed the parameters exceeding regulatory thresholds.

Methodology

Modelling geometry, testing envelope upgrades, and sizing systems

After defining thermal zones, wall constructions, and linear thermal bridges in Pléiades, I evaluated design upgrades across both the building fabric and mechanical systems.

  • Digitised building geometry, orientation, opaque/glazed assemblies, and thermal bridges in Pléiades.
  • Analysed baseline transmission and ventilation losses to target high-impact fabric improvements.
  • Implemented a hybrid internal/external insulation strategy (ITI/ITE), cutting the Bbio indicator by 39%.
  • Sized space-heating, ventilation, and domestic hot water systems, integrating a solar thermal water heater (CESI).
  • Verified summer thermal comfort (Tic) and all mandatory RT2012 safeguard criteria.

Results

Full RT2012 compliance and simulated EPC rating

Combining hybrid ITI/ITE insulation with a solar thermal water heater brings all regulatory metrics comfortably below RT2012 caps. The resulting French Energy Performance Certificate (DPE) label summarises the building’s energy and carbon rating.

Full regulatory coefficients (Bbio, Cep, Tic), heat-loss breakdowns, and variant comparisons are documented in the technical report.

Deliverables

A comprehensive thermal compliance report

The report compiles boundary conditions, wall/glazing schedules, comparative insulation trade-offs, and official Pléiades regulatory calculation sheets.
